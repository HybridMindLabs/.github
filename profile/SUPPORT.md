# Support

## Getting Help

- **Bug reports** — open an issue on the relevant repository (HybridCore core,
  or the specific extension you're using).
- **Questions / general help** — email
  [support@hybridmarket.org](mailto:support@hybridmarket.org).
<!-- - **Community / real-time help** — join our Discord: <link> -->

## Before You Report a Bug

To help us fix it faster, please include:

- HybridCore version (Admin → Update, or `extension.json`'s `version` field)
- The extension and its version, if the issue is extension-specific
- Steps to reproduce
- Any relevant error message (check `storage/logs/laravel.log`)

## Licensed Extensions

Player Stats and HybridGuard require a license. If you're having trouble with
license activation, include your license key in your support email (never
post it publicly in an issue).
